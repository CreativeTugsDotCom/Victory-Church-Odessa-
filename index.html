Saving souls with our skills.

"Our commitment is to exalt the name of our Lord Jesus, to proclaim the greatness of the Kingdom of God, to teach the truth of the Holy Bible, to encourage believers to devote their lives to serve our Lord, and to provide tools for leaders to develop mission fields and become organized churches — all with the intention of saving souls."

Founded in September 2016 by Dr. Gian C. Villatoro and a devoted company of believers in Odessa, Texas. Victory Church represents a multi-faceted digital and physical outreach combining systematic teaching, musical arts, and technical development.

Dr. Villatoro brings a broad background spanning theology, Christian counseling, software engineering, writing, musical composition, and television production to the ministry.
